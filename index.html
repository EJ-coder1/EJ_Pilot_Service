FAQ

Common questions.

How do I place an order?

Build the order using the form above — set your ranks, choose a hero, add your details — then hit "Send to Messenger" to send the generated order directly to us.

How does the pilot process work?

After confirming your order details and final price through Messenger, you share the account access needed to play ranked games. The pilot plays matches on your behalf using your preferred hero and role.

Can I choose any hero?

Yes. The picker lists all 133 heroes in the current dataset. Confirm your preferred hero and any specific conditions before the order starts.

Are the website prices final?

No. The calculator is an estimate only. Always confirm the actual total, schedule and conditions through Messenger before paying anything.

When do I pay?

Payment is made only after the final quote is confirmed via Messenger. Never pay before confirming price and schedule.

How long does an order take?

Timing depends on rank tier, stars required, queue conditions and schedule. Confirm expected timing during the Messenger conversation.

What information should I NOT send?

Never send your account password, OTP/one-time PIN, recovery code, or GCash/payment PIN to anyone — including through Messenger.

How do I receive updates?

All updates and communication are handled directly through Messenger. Keep notifications on so you don't miss any status updates during the order.

What is the Global vs Philippines MMR difference?

Global MMR means your account competes against international players across all servers. This tends to be more competitive than the Philippine server pool, so Global boosting carries a higher surcharge.

Why does hero difficulty affect the price?

High-difficulty heroes — Fanny, Ling, Gusion, Chou, etc. — require precise mechanics and deeper game knowledge. This increases piloting effort significantly, which is reflected in the price.
