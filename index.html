FAQ

Questions customers ask.

How do I place an order?

Build the order, choose a hero, review the estimate, then use the Messenger button to send the generated details.

Can I choose any hero?

Yes. The picker lists the current hero dataset available to the site. Confirm the preferred hero and final order with EJ Pilot Service before payment.

Are the website prices final?

No. The calculator is an estimate. Confirm the actual total, schedule and service conditions before paying.

Does the website need my password?

No. The public form intentionally does not ask for your password, OTP, recovery code or payment PIN.
