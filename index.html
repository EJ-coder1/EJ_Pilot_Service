Topic
FAQ

Common questions.

How do I place an order?

Build the order, choose your MMR and payment method, then submit it. The site gives you an Order ID for status checks. Messenger can still be used for final order discussion.

How does the pilot process work?

After confirming your order details and final price through Messenger, you share the account access needed to play ranked games. The pilot plays matches on your behalf using your preferred hero and role.

Can I choose any hero?

Yes. The picker lists all 133 heroes in the current dataset. Confirm your preferred hero and any specific conditions before the order starts.

Are the website prices final?

The calculator uses the fixed rates shown on this page. Confirm the final quote and schedule for your specific order before payment.

When do I pay?

Pay only after the admin approves your order. The selected payment details remain hidden until that approval.

How long does an order take?

Timing depends on rank tier, stars required, queue conditions and schedule. Confirm expected timing during the Messenger conversation.

What information should I NOT send?

Never send your account password, OTP/one-time PIN, recovery code, or GCash/payment PIN to anyone — including through Messenger.

How do I receive updates?

Use your Order ID to check the order and payment status. Keep your Order ID private.

What is the Global vs Philippines MMR difference?

PH and Global are separate MMR targets. The site uses fixed per-star MMR rates instead of a percentage surcharge, and the user chooses the target server.

Does hero difficulty add another fee?

Yes. Selecting a high-difficulty hero (marked ★ — examples: Fanny, Ling, Hayabusa, Gusion, Lancelot, Benedetta, Beatrix, Harith, Atlas, Arlott) adds +₱8 per star/point on top of the base rate. Normal heroes have no extra fee. The estimate updates live in the order builder the moment you pick a hero.

How does Top Rank pricing work?

Pick PH or Global MMR, then optionally choose a Top Rank target. Extra fixed fees apply per star/point: Top 1 +₱15, Top 10 +₱10, Top 50 +₱6, Top 100 +₱4. The estimate updates live in the order builder.
